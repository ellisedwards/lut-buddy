Your footage, every look

A project can mix clips from multiple cameras. Each clip has its own recording curve and gamut; its scenes inherit that profile. Choose matching input and Rec.709 output profiles for imported LUTs in Library. Profiles are supplied by you: a CUBE file does not reliably identify them.

The local service copies imported footage into your library, decodes marked original frames and preserves your original CUBE files. Footage and storage stay local. Optional AI suggestions send selected previews and details to your chosen provider. Saved scenes keep working without the original card.

Your migrated gallery keeps its existing previews, favourites and adjustments. Newly imported LUTs are applied directly when profiles match. Linked camera versions keep the same look selected across compatible scenes. Profile-adapted looks use published colour math; physical camera matching and calibrated display colour remain [Unverified]. Use Device in the scene picker to browse one camera or all devices. Exposure and relative source balance use published curves for supported recording profiles. Confirm unknown profiles before using those controls. Contrast and saturation remain available.

Favourites and adjustments save automatically. Library → Save project downloads footage, scenes, collections, LUTs and adjustments in one project file. Open project and Restore backup open separate copies. Undo / Redo in Library or ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z reverse names, descriptions, profiles, collection edits, ordering and scene removal. Use Back up library for a copy of the entire library; the buttons below export and restore viewer settings only.
